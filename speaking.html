A practitioner's point of view

My talks come from work with financial institutions across Europe, the Middle East, and the United States—not from speculation about a distant future.

I focus on the choices behind the technology: where AI creates leverage, where it creates fragility, and what leaders must protect as institutions learn to think and act faster.

Three conversations worth having

Each topic is adapted to the audience, the institution, and the decision in the room.

AI in Financial Services

From pilot to operating model

Why most banking AI efforts stop at demonstration—and how workflow ownership, governance, data access, and procurement determine what reaches production.

For leaders deciding where to invest, what to change, and how to separate durable capability from hype.

One Brain, Many Minds

Shared intelligence without groupthink

What happens when an institution can remember, reason, and act as one system—and why conscience, dissent, attention, and human dignity matter more as coordination improves.

For audiences exploring the human meaning of AI, beyond productivity and model performance.

Europe ↔ Silicon Valley

Speed, trust, and two innovation cultures

Why American technology playbooks often fail inside European finance, what Europe underestimates about speed, and how regulation can become an operating advantage.

For teams building, buying, or scaling financial technology across the Atlantic.
