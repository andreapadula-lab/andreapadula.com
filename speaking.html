Perspective

Experience first. Hype last.

My talks come from work with financial institutions across Europe, the Middle East, and the United States—not from speculation about a distant future.

I focus on the decisions behind the technology: where AI creates leverage, where it creates fragility, and how leaders move from a compelling pilot to an accountable operating model.
