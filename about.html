Journey Across contexts Europe, Middle East, Asia, US

A career shaped by context.

I work between financial institutions and the teams building what comes next. The path formed a point of view that is still in motion.

Current Head of Partnerships & Business Development, Streetbeat Europe, Middle East, United States
Experience The work Financial institutions × AI

Making two operating cultures understand each other.

I spend my time with banks, wealth managers, and financial leaders asking a practical question: where can AI create durable value without weakening trust, judgment, or accountability?

Financial institutions are built for continuity and control. Technology companies are built for speed and reinvention. My role is to connect the right people, translate the constraints, and create a path from possibility to production.

Working principles

Three ideas that travel.

Production before theatre.

A convincing demo is not an operating model. The real work begins with workflows, ownership, incentives, and deployment.

Context before scale.

Regulation, culture, and local trust determine whether an idea survives contact with the institution around it.

Accountability stays human.

AI can extend judgment, memory, and reach. It should make responsibility more visible—not make it disappear.
