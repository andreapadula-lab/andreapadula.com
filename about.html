The work behind the title

I spend my time with banks, wealth managers, and financial leaders asking a practical question: where can AI create durable value without weakening trust, judgment, or accountability?

That question sits between two worlds. Financial institutions are built for continuity and control. Technology companies are built for speed and reinvention. My work is to make those systems understand one another well enough to build something useful.

A career shaped by context

Six countries, three continents, and one recurring lesson: innovation only works when it understands the institution around it.

    Finance and strategy foundations

  2. Doha & Riyadh

  3. Taipei

  4. Milan ↔ Menlo Park

    Building the bridge

    Today I work from Milan with Streetbeat, whose headquarters are in Menlo Park, bringing financial AI into European and Middle Eastern institutions.

How I approach the work

Production before theatre

A convincing demo is not an operating model. The real work begins with workflows, ownership, incentives, and deployment.

Context before scale

Technology travels faster than institutions change. Regulation, culture, and local trust determine whether an idea survives contact with reality.

Accountability stays human

AI can extend judgment, memory, and reach. It should make responsibility more visible—not make it disappear.
