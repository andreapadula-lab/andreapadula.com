About

Head of Partnerships & Business Development at Streetbeat — an AI platform for financial services based in Menlo Park, California. Backed by CDP Venture Capital and Azimut.

I split my time between Palo Alto and Milan. My job is to bring AI into European banks and financial institutions — which means I spend most of my days in conversations with C-level executives about what AI can actually do for them, beyond the slide deck.

Before Streetbeat: ESCP Business School (110/110), roles across Milan, Paris, Doha, Riyadh, and Taipei. I speak Italian, English, French, and some Portuguese.

I write about what I see at the intersection of technology, innovation, and financial services. Not theory — field notes.
