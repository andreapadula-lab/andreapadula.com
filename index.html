About

Head of Partnerships & Business Development at Streetbeat — an AI platform for financial services based in Menlo Park, California.

I'm based in Milan and I work at the intersection of Silicon Valley innovation and European financial institutions. My job is to bring AI into banks, wealth managers, and asset managers across Europe and the Middle East — which means I spend my days in conversations with C-level executives about what AI can actually do for them, beyond the slide deck.

I've lived and worked in six countries across three continents: Italy, France, Qatar, Saudi Arabia, Taiwan, and the United States. I've worked in consulting (Deloitte), financial services, and tech — always at the crossroads of innovation and business development.

I write about what I see in the field. Not theory — observations from someone who sells AI to people who don't trust AI yet.
